Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how DAEHAN APP handles information when authorized personnel use its shipping, production, receiving, inventory, quality, and administration features.

Who handles your information

DAEHAN APP is used for internal business operations. Your organization administers employee accounts, department access, and operational records. Erik Lopez is the application support and privacy contact: eriklopez40@hotmail.com.

Information processed

Account information includes your name, email address, user identifier, role, department permissions, and account status. Authentication information is processed to sign you in and maintain your session. The app stores work records you enter, including activities, timestamps, products, quantities, machine parameters, notes, and the user associated with a record. Technical services may process IP addresses, request logs, app and device versions, and update requests necessary to operate and secure the service. Support messages include the information you choose to send.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate users, apply access permissions, record business activity, generate reports, maintain inventory and quality traceability, provide support, deliver app updates, and protect the service. Authorized supervisors and administrators can access records according to their permissions. DAEHAN APP does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

Service providers and disclosures

Supabase provides authentication and database services; Vercel hosts the web application and public pages; Expo provides mobile app updates. These providers process information needed to deliver their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Information may be processed outside your country. We share information only with authorized organization personnel and providers needed to operate the app, or when required by law or to protect the service.

Device storage and security

The app stores session information and preferences on your device to keep you signed in and remember settings. Signing out ends the local session; deleting the app does not delete server records. Connections use HTTPS and application access is controlled through accounts and permissions. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials private and contact support if you suspect unauthorized access.

Retention and deletion

Account information is kept while needed to provide authorized access. Operational records may remain after an account is deactivated to preserve production, inventory, shipment, quality, and audit history. Retention depends on the organization's operational needs and applicable obligations; there is no single fixed period for all records. Information no longer needed is deleted or anonymized where feasible. Deactivating an account is different from deleting personal information.

Your requests and choices

Contact your organization's administrator or email eriklopez40@hotmail.com to request access, correction, account closure, deletion of personal information, or withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. Include the account email and your request, but never your password. We verify identity and coordinate with your organization before acting. Some business records may need to be retained; we will explain any applicable limitation. You may stop using the app and sign out at any time.

Children and policy updates

DAEHAN APP is intended for authorized organizational personnel and is not directed to children under 13. Privacy questions or concerns can be sent to the contact below. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
