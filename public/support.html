Report a technical issue

To help us investigate, include:

  • The department and screen where the issue occurred.
  • What you were doing and any error message.
  • Your device model, app version, and when it happened.
  • A screenshot, if helpful, with confidential information hidden.

Do not send passwords or sensitive operational information by email. This support page is available without signing in.
